4. HOW THE PACKAGE ('pybasics_kit') IS BEING USED
================================================================================

Cashly delegates all financial and mathematical computations to the external
library `pybasics_kit`, keeping database storage decoupled from business math.

The package is imported and used in the following files:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. finances/services/calculator.py
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the primary mathematical engine. It imports 12 functions from `pybasics_kit`:

1. pybasics_kit.calculate_balance(income, expenses)
   - Used in: compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Computes Net Balance (Income minus Expenses).

2. pybasics_kit.calculate_savings(income, expenses)
   - Used in: compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Computes positive savings delta.

3. pybasics_kit.calculate_savings_rate(income, savings)
   - Used in: compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Computes savings as a percentage of total income.

4. pybasics_kit.calculate_total_expenses(expenses)
   - Used in: compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Aggregates total monthly expense totals.

5. pybasics_kit.calculate_percentage_change(old, new)
   - Used in: compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Computes month-over-month growth or reduction (+/- %) for income and expenses.

6. pybasics_kit.calculate_budget_usage(spending, budget)
   - Used in: compute_budget_details() and compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Computes percentage of budget consumed so far.

7. pybasics_kit.calculate_budget_remaining(budget, spending)
   - Used in: compute_budget_details() and compute_savings_goal_details()
   - Purpose: Computes leftover budget allowance, and remaining amount to hit savings goals.

8. pybasics_kit.calculate_daily_budget(remaining, days)
   - Used in: compute_budget_details() and compute_financial_summary()
   - Purpose: Divides remaining budget by remaining days in the month to calculate daily spending allowance.

9. pybasics_kit.calculate_percentage(value, total)
   - Used in: compute_category_breakdown() and compute_savings_goal_details()
   - Purpose: Calculates each category's share of total spending for the donut chart and savings goal completion %.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. finances/views.py (Line 373)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Used in: reports_view()
- Functions used:
    calculate_balance(income, expenses)
    calculate_savings(income, expenses)
    calculate_savings_rate(income, savings)
- Purpose: Aggregates the 12-month annual totals in the Annual Financial Report page.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. finances/tests.py (Lines 8-12)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Used in: PyBasicsKitIntegrationTests
- Verifies that all calculations run accurately against Django model instances.

5. PERMISSION MATRIX & ROLE SWITCHING
================================================================================

Roles defined in `accounts/permissions.py`:

- Standard User:
  Can view/edit/delete own transactions, budgets, goals. Cannot see other users.

- Financial Manager:
  All Standard capabilities + Audit All Transactions + Override Limits + Delete Category.

- Auditor:
  Read-only view of all transactions and budgets + CSV Export clearance.

- Viewer:
  Read-only view of personal dashboard without modification permissions.

*To switch your role for testing:*
Visit http://127.0.0.1:8000/auth/profile/, pick a role under "Role Assignment", and click Save.
